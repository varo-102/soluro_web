## Brand & Style

This design system targets commercial operators, field representatives, independent contractors, and nimble retail merchants who require high-velocity mobile point-of-sale efficiency, offline-first reliability, and institutional trust. 

The aesthetic is **Corporate Modern with Tactile Warmth**:
- **Confidence & Rigor:** Deep midnight navy conveys financial-grade security, enterprise stability, and architectural precision.
- **Energy & Agility:** Sunburst gold injects immediate actionable clarity into primary conversion targets, QR countdown alerts, and financial interactions.
- **Warm Editorial Grounding:** Natural off-white and cream foundations eliminate digital glare during outdoor or point-of-sale field operations, delivering a clean, paper-grade feel without sterility.
- **Contrast & Utility:** Precision line-weights, dense data-to-ink ratios for invoices and QR workflows, and tactile rounded surfaces combine into an intuitive, zero-latency interface.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative midnight depth with high-visibility solar warmth:

- **Primary (`#0B1E36`):** Deepest midnight navy. Used for core brand touchpoints, authoritative action bars, dominant heading typography, and key interactive touch targets.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm solar gold. Governs critical interactive drivers: primary floating action triggers, countdown indicators, QR expiration warnings, and active operational states.
- **Tertiary (`#1E3A8A`):** Vivid financial cobalt. Powers link states, directional metadata accents, active navigational tabs, and system focus rings.
- **Neutral (`#1A1C1C`):** High-legibility soot black for text, paired with neutral utility grays (`#374151` and `#5F6368`) for subtext and contextual hints.
- **Surface Strategy:** The system employs a dual-surface methodology: `#FAF8F2` forms the tactile page ground, while `#FFFFFF` elevates individual operational cards and data tiles with crisp perimeter containment.

## Typography

The typography couples geometric commercial headings with systematic transactional clarity:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Crafted with balanced curves, geometric confidence, and tight negative tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to deliver modern, punchy executive titles across landing screens and dashboard views.
- **Body & Numerical Data (Inter):** Highly legible vertical metrics, neutral grotesque structure, and tall x-height designed for readability in dynamic field environments and numerical tables (currencies, totals, serial identifiers).
- **Tabular Figures:** All price tags, invoice calculation tables, and expiration timers must utilize monospace numeric variants (`font-feature-settings: "tnum"`) to guarantee flawless vertical decimal alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined 8px modular cadence optimized for both mobile handheld ergonomics and responsive desktop reading:

- **Grid Architecture:** 
  - Desktop web and tablet views execute on a 12-column layout with a maximum container limit of `1180px` for marketing and `860px` for transactional document viewports.
  - Mobile runs single-column stack streams with `16px` outer gutters.
- **Rhythm & Safe Areas:** Touch boundaries strictly preserve a `44px` minimum hit footprint. Mobile forms, invoice listings, and directory cards default to `16px` internal padding with `12px` gaps between sequential items.
- **Vertical Hierarchy:** Section divisions deploy `space-2xl` (`48px`) or `space-3xl` (`64px`) to preserve breathing space above card groupings.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered structural surfaces, tactile border boundaries, and soft ambient light falloffs:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground):** `#FAF8F2` provides a low-strain natural canvas background.
- **Level 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by a crisp hairline border (`1px solid #E5E7EB`) with an ultra-diffused atmospheric shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** Elevated cards, dropdown popovers, and quick-action sheets utilize `0 10px 25px -4px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Sheets & QR Focus Viewers):** Full focus overlays apply backdrop blurs (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) with deep ambient drop shadows `0 20px 35px -8px rgba(11, 30, 54, 0.16)`.
- **Accent Elevation:** Golden interactive controls (FABs, primary timers) carry a warm tinted ambient spread: `0 4px 14px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

## Shapes

The system uses balanced squircle curvature reflecting modern enterprise mobile ergonomics:

- **Cards & Data Modules:** `16px` (`rounded-xl`) outer corner radiuses provide a clean, modern frame for quotes and QR screens.
- **Buttons & Form Fields:** `12px` (`rounded-lg`) creates comfortable, accessible tap targets.
- **Pills, Badges & Micro-tags:** `9999px` full-stadium rounding for status indicators (e.g., "Aprobado", "Vence en 12:45").
- **Structural Accents:** 4px vertical anchor bars in `#1E3A8A` or `#F59E0B` run flush along section headers to reinforce architectural alignment.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Navy:** Solid `#0B1E36` fill with pure white typography, `12px` border radius, `12px 24px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#12284C`.
- **Action Solar (CTA):** Solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#0B1E36` text, font weight `700`, with subtle glow shadow `0 4px 14px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`. Active state triggers `#D97706`.
- **Secondary Outlined:** Pure white or transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#0B1E36`, navy text, transitioning to `#EFF6FF` on press.
- **Full-Width Mobile Sticky:** Fixed bottom dock button, height `54px`, high contrast, pill or `12px` corner radius.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Pending / Timer:** Golden pill background `#FEF3C7`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`, text `#B45309`.
- **Approved / Success:** Clean emerald pill background `#F0FDF4`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`, text `#15803D`.
- **Category / Neutral:** Tinted slate background `#EFF6FF`, border `1px solid #DBEAFE`, text `#1D4ED8`.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `12px`, padding `0 16px`. Active focus state drives border to `#1E3A8A` with a subtle 3px ring (`rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.12)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `20px` touch frame, border `#CBD5E1`, filled with `#0B1E36` when selected, carrying a crisp white vector icon check.

### Data & Quote Cards
- Surface `#FFFFFF`, hairline border `#E5E7EB`, padding `20px`, rounded `16px`. Internal layout presents clear visual separation between metadata headers, customer details, and price breakdowns.

### QR Display Viewport
- Pure white container with high-contrast interior padding (`24px`), central QR matrix rendering, accompanied by a dynamic countdown pill banner and quick-action clipboard button (`#0B1E36`).
